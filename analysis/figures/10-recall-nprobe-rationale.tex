All IVF schemes share the same IVF index (\texttt{n\_centroids =
ceil($\sqrt{N}$)}, \texttt{train\_seed=42},
\texttt{max\_iter=25}; an architectural invariant in
\texttt{CLAUDE.md}), so their recall vs nprobe curves track
identically --- divergence here would be a bug, not a property.
The chart's value is reading off ``how aggressive does the probe
need to be to reach $X$\,\% recall'', independent of any
scheme-specific overhead. Combine with figure 01 to convert the
nprobe answer into a latency cost for any specific scheme.
