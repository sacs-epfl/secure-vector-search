Three panels grouped by communication shape, sharing a single
log-scale y-axis so bar heights are directly comparable across
panels. \textsf{Online-only} (left): plaintext / \sap carry only the
latency-critical round-trip --- query~$\uparrow$ and
response~$\downarrow$, no setup or offline traffic. \textsf{With
one-time setup} (middle): the cryptographic schemes that encrypt
the corpus matrix once at upload time pay an $m \times n$ Setup
cost (the green bar), amortised across every subsequent query.
\textsf{With per-query offline traffic} (right): \tiptoe's BFV
query token (Offline~$\uparrow$, $\sim$32\,MB/query) and the
matching apply-hint download (Offline~$\downarrow$) are paid
\emph{per query} on every request and don't amortise --- the
structural reason fully-cryptographic schemes haven't yet hit
deployment budgets.
