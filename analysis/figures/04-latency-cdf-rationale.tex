The CDF shape at the operating point distinguishes ``predictable
latency'' (steep, near-vertical curves) from ``long tails''
(curves that flatten before reaching CDF~=~1). Each scheme's
curve is sourced from the quality\_param closest to 90\,\% recall,
so cross-scheme comparison is at \emph{matched} recall, not
matched config. A scheme whose curve sits to the right at every
percentile pays its latency cost everywhere, not just on the
mean.
