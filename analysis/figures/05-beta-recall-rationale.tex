At $\beta = 0$ \sap is rank-equivalent to plaintext (the
perturbation is zero), so the leftmost data point matches the
plaintext ceiling line. Each step rightward trades recall for
differential privacy ($\epsilon$-DP shrinks as $\beta$ grows).
The slope tells you how fast that trade-off bites: a sharp drop
means the \sap construction is recall-fragile at this corpus, a
gentle drop means there's headroom to spend on $\beta$ before the
ranking collapses.
