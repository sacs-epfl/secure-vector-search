The \textsf{verify} segment is the Protocol~2 substep alone --- a
narrower scope than the \textsf{on}/\textsf{off} gap in
Fig.~\ref{fig:13a-bntm-recall-latency}. A non-zero \textsf{side

when material, it indicates verification has compute-path effects
beyond the verify substep itself. A retune of the sparsity parameter
$\mu$ would shift the trade-off but is out of scope here.
