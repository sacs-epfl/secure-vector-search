The gap between an \textsf{off} marker and its \textsf{on}
counterpart is the end-to-end wall-clock verification overhead --- it
includes side effects (RNG warm-up, branch prediction state) on top of
the Protocol~2 substep itself. The Sec128-target trial count is
$\lambda' = 3$; halving $\lambda'$ would roughly halve the gap but also
weaken the malicious-server detection bound.
