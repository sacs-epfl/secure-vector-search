Normalising to 100\,\% removes the absolute-scale axis so the
\emph{shape} of per-query cost is directly comparable across
schemes that differ by orders of magnitude. A scheme dominated
by \textsf{server-compute} is parallelism-friendly (cross-reference
figure 07's parallel scaling); a scheme with a fat
\textsf{decompress} or \textsf{decode} segment has its tax on the
client side, where threading helps less.
