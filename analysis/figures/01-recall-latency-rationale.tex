Read the chart as ``at this latency budget, what recall do I get?''
The x-axis is log-scaled, so the leftmost ten-fold step (1\,ms vs
10\,ms) takes the same horizontal space as the rightmost step
(100\,ms vs 1\,s) --- visually, the cryptographic schemes' shift
right is much larger than the markers suggest. Error bars are
across-rep only --- they show measurement stability under repeated
identical runs, not the spread across the query distribution. Cross-reference figure 04 (latency CDF) for tail
behaviour at a single operating point and figure 03 (communication)
for the bytes-per-query side of the trade-off.
