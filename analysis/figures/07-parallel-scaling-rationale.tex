The vertical guide at $N{=}16$ is the \emph{socket boundary} on
sacs006's dual-socket Xeon Gold 6426Y --- past this thread count
NUMA crossing dominates and remote-memory traffic eats the
parallel gain. The guide at $N{=}32$ is the SMT boundary: past
that, threads share execution units with their physical sibling
and ``parallelism'' is logical-only. Solid lines are pinned to
socket~0 (\texttt{physcpubind=0-15,membind=0}); dashed are
unpinned (16, 32, 64) so the reader can attribute slope changes
to the right phenomenon. \tiptoe's flat line is the headline
finding for the index $\times$ hardware story: BFV is
single-threaded, so adding cores cannot rescue \tiptoe's
throughput.
